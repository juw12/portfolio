Engineering

Bachelor Thesis: Preconditioned CFD for a Supersonic Nozzle

Derived a Weiss–Smith preconditioner for the 1-D Euler equations and coded a Fortran MUSCL–Roe nozzle solver.

Bachelor research project, Aerospace Engineering, Tamkang University (Jan 2022, advisor Dr. Yang-Yao Niu). Derived the eigenvalues and flux-difference splitting of a time-derivative (Weiss–Smith) preconditioned system for the quasi-1-D Euler equations, then implemented it in a Fortran finite-volume solver (MUSCL reconstruction with Roe’s approximate Riemann solver). Validated on a diverging nozzle with supersonic inflow and both supersonic and subsonic outflow, comparing Mach number, pressure, density, temperature and velocity against the analytical solution and the Roe, Steger–Warming and Van Leer schemes.
CFD Fortran Euler equations MUSCL / Roe preconditioning
