The short version

I'm Juwen. At Pegatron in Batam I take IP cameras and CCTV housings from EVT to mass production: sealing them to IPX6/7, running DVT, and chasing every failure back to its root cause.

Before that I studied aerospace engineering at Tamkang University in Taiwan, graduating with a 3.85 GPA and a thesis where I wrote a CFD solver for nozzle flow.

After hours I build the tools I wish I had at work: Stackup for tolerance stack-ups, the ME R&D Toolkit for NPI, and a library of 3D models. On weekends I make progressive house.

If you build physical products, or want to, we should talk.
