# Update - mint (3.1: language & region first, apps for Windows, Mac and Android)

- When: 2026-10-29 20:10
- What: Mint 3.1. The first visit asks for a language (English, Bahasa Indonesia, 简体中文, Español) and a region; the region sets the currency of new budgets and templates, and both can be changed later in Settings. Mint now downloads as a real app on Windows (installer or portable zip), Mac (Apple chip and Intel) and Android (opens on Budget, Back button, haptics, backups / CSV / result cards through the share sheet), alongside the iPhone home-screen app and the web. The old name is gone everywhere: saved budgets move to Mint's own storage once, shared budgets sync on Mint relay topics, and the separate Kitty notes were removed from this vault.
