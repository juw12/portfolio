# Update - kitty (new app)

- When: 2026-10-28 23:00
- What: New shared-budgeting app **Kitty**: a live, end-to-end encrypted money sheet for couples and housemates; a 14-question onboarding quiz that assigns one of six money personalities and plans the budget from income; limit warnings (threshold, over, pace) with occasional persona tips; goals; and an investing portfolio with allocation drift, rebalancing and a growth projection. Includes a self-hostable stdlib relay.
