# Update - teman-sehat 1.0 (new app)

- When: 2026-11-01 00:24
- What: New app Teman Sehat, a GERD lifestyle companion in Bahasa Indonesia that also covers dieting and fitness without fasting and acne care. Hari Ini summary with an after-meal timer (stay upright 3 h, hard exercise after about 2 h), editable daily reminders, a symptom and trigger log, calorie and protein targets split into five small meals with a GERD-friendly Indonesian food log, exercise and weight tracking, a skin-routine checklist with a pimple counter, and guides with warning signs. Installable offline PWA for desktop, Android and iPhone; Capacitor shell with native daily reminders; `#demo` sample data in separate storage.

- Demo: https://juw12.github.io/projects/teman-sehat/index.html#demo
