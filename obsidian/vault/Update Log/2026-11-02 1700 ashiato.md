# Update - ashiato

- When: 2026-11-02 17:00
- What: New app. Ashiato (足跡, "footprints") is a cozy photo diary, walking map and weather town for iPhone in a Japanese/Korean indie style, combining ideas from One Good Thing, DOOD, DayCity, Kiroku, PlainWalk, Footnotes, echor and Clutter. Each day is a card in a slow 3D swipe deck: a three.js street-corner diorama lit by that day's weather and time (192 pre-rendered scenes), a polaroid photo, a note, a hanko mood stamp and the distance walked. A screenshot tidy-up deck (keep / let go / later) sorts screenshots into drawers with on-device OCR (JA/KO/EN), duplicate detection and ticket-expiry flags. Four language modes: duo (Japanese and Korean side by side), English, Japanese, Korean.
- App: https://juw12.github.io/projects/ashiato/?lang=en (web prototype of the swipe deck)
- Tidy deck: https://juw12.github.io/projects/ashiato/tidy.html?lang=en
- Source: https://github.com/juw12/projects/tree/main/apps/ashiato
- Repos: juw12/projects (`apps/ashiato/`, catalog entry), juw12/portfolio (card), juw12/juw12 (profile, Building section), juw12/obsidian (notes)
- Checks: screenshot classifier 40/40 on tuning samples and 20/20 on hold-out (18/20 before two lexicon fixes), 60-case golden file for a Swift parity test; headless-Edge screenshots of the deck in English and duo and of the tidy deck mid-swipe and done; `check_site.py` reports no issues for the ashiato pages.
