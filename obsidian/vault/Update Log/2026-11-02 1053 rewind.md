# Update - rewind

- When: 2026-11-02 10:53
- What: New app. Rewind is a community archive for old TV series that are hard to find on today's streaming platforms: browse poster tiles with search and filters, series panels with memories, quotes, remember-counts and a community availability status, My Shelf, and a Memory Lane feed. v1.1 adds a full Settings page (theme System/Light/Dark, accent colour, text size, card density, reduce motion, display name, start page, default sort, hide already-streaming shows, export/import/reset), a day/night quick toggle, shareable hash links for every page and series, a share button, and offline/online notices.
- App: https://juw12.github.io/projects/rewind/ (local mode; data stays in the browser)
- Source: https://github.com/juw12/projects/tree/main/apps/rewind
- Repos: juw12/projects (`apps/rewind/`, catalog entry), juw12/portfolio (card), juw12/juw12 (profile, Building section), juw12/obsidian (notes)
