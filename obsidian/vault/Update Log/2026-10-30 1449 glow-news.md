# Update - glow 1.28.0-1.28.1 (GlowNews: the day's AI news, twice a day)

- When: 2026-10-30 14:49
- What: Glow has a fourth app, GlowNews (glow/news/): the day's AI news in one place with a new edition twice a day (00:00 and 12:00 UTC = 07:00 and 19:00 WIB). It reads official lab blogs (OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Google AI, Hugging Face, NVIDIA, AWS, Meta), tech outlets (TechCrunch, The Verge, Ars Technica, MIT Technology Review, Wired, VentureBeat, The Decoder, MarkTechPost), Simon Willison, Import AI, Latent Space, Hacker News and Hugging Face daily papers. The same story from several outlets becomes one card with every source; stories are ranked by source quality, coverage, votes and freshness and tagged Models / Research / Tools & apps / Business / Policy & safety / Open source. Glow's free no-sign-in AI (OVHcloud, then LLM7) writes 2-sentence summaries, a "why it matters" line and a 5-point briefing, all marked AI and linked to the publishers. Topic chips, search, New for you, saved stories, share and Ask Glow (opens GlowChat with the story and its sources). 1.28.1 renames it from AI News to GlowNews and gives it its own tile on Glow home next to GlowChat, GlowRoom and GlowLead, plus an "AI news" choice under the start box that opens GlowNews searching for what you typed. Glow home also shows the top 3 stories ("GlowNews - Today in AI").
- App: https://juw12.github.io/projects/glow/news/
- Repos: juw12/projects (`apps/glow`, `apps/glow/tools/ai_news.py`, Pages workflow, `scripts/apps.json`), juw12/portfolio (card copy + notes), juw12/juw12 (profile line)
- Checks: news builder selftest (story grouping, ranking, topics, half-day slots); a live edition built from 15 of 20 sources with 22 AI summaries and an AI briefing; all 11 static Glow test suites plus the new GlowNews test; site build and site check; browser checks on desktop and phone (60 cards, filters, search, saved stories, Glow apps menu, Glow home tile and "AI news" search hand-off, no console errors, no sideways scrolling); live version 1.28.1.
- How the twice-a-day refresh works: Pages rebuilds every hour; the builder makes a new edition only when the live one is older than the latest half-day slot and otherwise reuses it, so summaries and "first seen" times carry over. If a refresh fails, the committed edition is deployed.
- Limits: summaries come from free open models and can be generic or wrong (every card links to the source); Anthropic and Meta AI publish no news feeds, so their news arrives through outlets; free AI and feeds can change limits or go down (the edition then ships with the feed's own text).
