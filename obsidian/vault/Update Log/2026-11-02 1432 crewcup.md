# Update - crewcup

- When: 2026-11-02 14:32
- What: New app. CrewCup is office coffee for teams in Indonesia and Southeast Asia: one-tap usual, solo or group coffee runs with held payments, company allowance, badge-QR pickup at partner cafés, café order board and cup labels, HR budgets, spend, fairness and audit log, company rewards, a loyalty program (points, tiers, coupons, café offers, achievements, referrals) and the CrewCup Network. v0.7 simplifies the UI: a clean header with one Demo button, 5 tabs instead of 7, a home screen built around "Order my usual", a 4-section Rewards tab, plainer wording and a fixed phone layout.
