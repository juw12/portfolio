# Update - pips 2.24.2 (auto battle)

- When: 2026-10-30 08:31
- What: Pips 2.24.2 lets arena skill battles play themselves. Switch on Auto (button in the battle, the A key, or the tick box before a fight) and the pet picks its own skills: it finishes off a weak opponent, heals when low, guards when nearly knocked out and saves Focus for long fights. Leave Auto off to press the skills yourself (or keys 1-3); you can switch mid-battle and the choice is remembered. Same day: 2.24.0 turn-based skill battles (pick 3 skills, compare level and gear, best of 1/3/5), 5 minigames and a 5-tab Hub; 2.24.1 single click only tickles the pet, double-click opens the Hub.
