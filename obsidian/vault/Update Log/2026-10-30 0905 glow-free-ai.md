# Update - glow 1.27.0-1.27.3 (built-in free AI that switches by itself)

- When: 2026-10-30 09:05
- What: Glow now has more free AI built in, with no key or account, like Puter: OVHcloud AI Endpoints (Llama 3.3 70B, GPT-OSS 120B, Qwen 3.5 397B, Mistral Small 3.2; 2 requests a minute for each model) and LLM7 (about 10 a minute) sit next to Glow Free. When the Puter allowance runs out or one free AI is busy or used up, the same message goes straight to the next one (Puter, OVHcloud, LLM7, Glow Free, then the AI on the device), and a used-up one is skipped for a minute. 1.27.1-1.27.3 hide the bring-your-own options for now: GlowChat's own keys (OpenRouter, Gemini, Groq, Mistral), Ollama and any OpenAI-compatible API, and GlowRoom's own providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Custom/Ollama). Saved keys stay stored; one switch per app brings them back (OWN_FREE_KEYS_SHOWN, OWN_AI_SERVICES_SHOWN, OWN_PROVIDERS_SHOWN).
- App: https://juw12.github.io/projects/glow/
- Repos: juw12/projects (`apps/glow`, `scripts/apps.json`), juw12/portfolio (card copy + notes), juw12/juw12 (profile line)
- Checks: every keyless service tested from the browser side (CORS and real chat and streaming requests); selftest covers the switch order, the no-waiting rule and the one-minute skip; all 18 local Glow test suites; GlowRoom browser check (a saved Ollama setup falls back to Glow AI with no provider fields); Glow regression checks, CI and Pages deploy green; live version 1.27.3.
- Limits: the anonymous tiers are counted per network, not per person, and the services can change their limits or model lists without notice (Glow then moves on to the next one). Checked and left out: AI Horde (slow volunteer queue), DeepInfra, mlvoca, Hack Club AI (need a key now or don't answer browsers).
