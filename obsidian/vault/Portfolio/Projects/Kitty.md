A budgeting app you can share like a Google Sheet, with an interface built only for money. A 2-minute <strong>onboarding quiz</strong> on needs, wants and behaviour assigns one of six money personalities (Squirrel, Owl, Butterfly, Lion, Dolphin, Turtle) and turns your take-home pay into needs / wants / savings / investing envelopes, adjusted for rent, debt and your emergency fund. Track spending in an editable <strong>sheet</strong> (sort, filter, CSV), watch the left-to-spend ring and pace chart, and get <strong>warnings</strong> at 80%, over the limit, or when your pace will overspend, including when someone else's edit tips a category. Persona-aware tips appear now and then. Goals, plus an <strong>investing portfolio</strong> with target allocation, drift, rebalancing trades and a growth projection. <strong>Sharing</strong> is local-first and end-to-end encrypted (AES-GCM, key in the link fragment) with cell-level merging, presence and an activity feed, over ntfy.sh or a self-hostable stdlib relay. <strong>Free on every platform</strong>: the web app, an iPhone home-screen app (plus an unsigned IPA for sideloading) and a signed Android APK, built by GitHub Actions with Capacitor around the same live site, with haptics, the native share sheet, the Android back button and invite deep links. Version 1.2 adds a cleaner, neutral design system, <strong>four languages</strong> (English, Bahasa Indonesia, 简体中文, Español; auto-detected, switchable in Settings) and a shareable money-animal result card. Version 1.3 makes it simpler: a <strong>30-second setup</strong> (name, take-home pay, who it is for) builds a ready budget with the quiz now optional, a <strong>Getting started</strong> checklist guides the first steps, and new expenses are <strong>auto-categorised</strong> from your history and everyday words in four languages. Version 1.4 is calmer and easier on the eyes: a one-tap <strong>light / dark theme</strong> (or follow the system) on every screen with live previews in Settings, a phone tab bar with a big add button and a <strong>More</strong> sheet, bottom-sheet dialogs you can swipe away, tappable overview figures, search-as-you-type in the sheet and keyboard shortcuts. Version 1.5 keeps itself up to date: when a new version is live, the app says so (toast, bell, Settings) and updates in place with one tap, or by itself the next time you switch away, keeping your budgets and the page you were on, then shows what's new in your language; the iPhone and Android apps update the same way. No account and no build step: plain HTML/CSS/JS.
