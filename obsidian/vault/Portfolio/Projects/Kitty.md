A budgeting app you can share like a Google Sheet, with an interface built only for money. A 2-minute <strong>onboarding quiz</strong> on needs, wants and behaviour assigns one of six money personalities (Squirrel, Owl, Butterfly, Lion, Dolphin, Turtle) and turns your take-home pay into needs / wants / savings / investing envelopes, adjusted for rent, debt and your emergency fund. Track spending in an editable <strong>sheet</strong> (sort, filter, CSV), watch the left-to-spend ring and pace chart, and get <strong>warnings</strong> at 80%, over the limit, or when your pace will overspend, including when someone else's edit tips a category. Persona-aware tips appear now and then. Goals, plus an <strong>investing portfolio</strong> with target allocation, drift, rebalancing trades and a growth projection. <strong>Sharing</strong> is local-first and end-to-end encrypted (AES-GCM, key in the link fragment) with cell-level merging, presence and an activity feed, over ntfy.sh or a self-hostable stdlib relay. No account and no build step: plain HTML/CSS/JS.
