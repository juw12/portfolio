Ashiato (足跡, “footprints”) is a calm <strong>photo diary, walking map and weather town</strong> in one iPhone app, in a cozy Japanese/Korean indie style. Each day becomes a card in a slow <strong>3D swipe deck</strong>: a tiny three.js street-corner <strong>diorama</strong> lit by that day’s real weather and time (192 pre-rendered scenes), a polaroid photo, a short note, a hanko mood stamp and the distance you walked, with new streets marked on your personal <strong>walk map</strong>. On-this-day memories, random fragments and gentle daily prompts bring old days back. A <strong>screenshot tidy-up</strong> layer swipes through your screenshots (keep, let go, later), reads them on-device with OCR in Japanese, Korean and English, sorts them into drawers (tickets, receipts, recipes, places, chats), finds duplicates and flags expiring tickets. <strong>Four language modes</strong>: Japanese and Korean side by side, English, Japanese or Korean. The web prototype runs in the browser; the SwiftUI app (SwiftData, WeatherKit, PhotoKit, Vision, widgets) is written but not compiled yet.
