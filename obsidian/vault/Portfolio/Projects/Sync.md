<strong>Sync</strong> lets friends hear the same song at the same second from anywhere, with no shared Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube listeners share one room, each hearing it on their own service. You can also share your own audio files peer-to-peer, and voice chat lowers the music while friends talk. The Android app keeps playing in the background and with the screen off, with lock-screen controls; on iPhone it installs from Safari to the home screen. No account or server: rooms run device-to-device over WebRTC.
