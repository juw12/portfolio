<strong>Sync</strong> lets friends hear the same song at the same second from anywhere, with no shared Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube listeners share one room, each hearing it on their own service. You can also share your own audio files peer-to-peer, and voice chat lowers the music while friends talk. No account or server: rooms run browser-to-browser over WebRTC.
