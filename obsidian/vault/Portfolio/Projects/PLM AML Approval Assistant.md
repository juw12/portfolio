Python + Playwright assistant for a Windchill-based PLM part-approval (AML) workflow: searches the part, starts the workflow, assigns the project, creates or re-links the approval documents, fills the final or conditional approval form (customer-assigned or not), adds the reviewer, verifies every field and <strong>stops before Submit</strong>. Four one-click launchers and a step-by-step training deck for new users. v0.1 preliminary release.
