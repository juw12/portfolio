Lifestyle companion for people with GERD, in Bahasa Indonesia, that also covers dieting without fasting and acne care. <strong>Hari Ini</strong> brings it together: the next reminder, a daily summary (kcal, protein, exercise, GERD habits, skin routine, symptoms, pimples) and an after-meal timer (“stay upright 3 h, hard exercise after about 2 h”). <strong>Pengingat</strong>: an editable daily schedule for GERD habits, exercise and the morning/night skin routine. <strong>Gejala</strong>: a symptom log with triggers and a 30-day trigger ranking. <strong>Fit</strong>: calorie and protein targets (Mifflin–St Jeor, Asian BMI cut-offs, a calorie floor) split into five small meals, a food log of GERD-friendly Indonesian foods with trigger foods flagged, an exercise log against 150 min cardio plus 2–3 strength days, and a weight chart with a goal line. <strong>Kulit</strong>: a skin-routine checklist, a 14-day pimple counter and a guide (actives, non-BPOM creams to avoid, whey and high-GI links, and the doxycycline warning for GERD). Installable PWA for desktop, Android and iPhone that works offline; a Capacitor build schedules reminders as native notifications so they fire with the app closed. Local-only, no account, with a clear not-medical-advice note.

## Install

- **Desktop (Windows/Mac/Linux)** — open the app in Edge/Chrome → *Install* icon in the address bar; offline launcher `apps/teman-sehat/desktop/Buka-Aplikasi-Desktop.cmd`.
- **Android** — Chrome → ⋮ → *Install app*.
- **iPhone** — Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen* (notifications need iOS 16.4+).
- **Native iOS/Android** — Capacitor shell in `apps/teman-sehat/mobile/` (Node 22+, Android Studio / Xcode); schedules every reminder as an OS notification so it fires with the app closed.

Not medical advice: a lifestyle reminder and guide, not a substitute for a doctor, skin doctor or dietitian.
