Downloadable Windows console (v1.11.17) with a locally bundled controller, serial port chooser, macros and offline demo. Remote by code lets a phone or another PC control the AIRIZ and see its camera through this PC with a 6-digit code, and a PIN-protected on-device Bluetooth remote adds wireless pan, up/down, LEDs and a real motor stop from Android or iPhone (Bluefy). The dashboard shows real device facts: the firmware the AIRIZ reports, a Bluetooth self-test result, live link activity, a camera check that confirms each jog really moved the camera, automatic aim and timing calibration from the camera picture, and a read-only hardware check report. Camera tools add presets, motion detection, a siren and talk / listen through the AIRIZ speaker and microphone. The browser and mobile-web editions remain available.
