Downloadable Windows console with a locally bundled controller, serial port chooser, camera tools, macros and offline demo. A PIN-protected on-device Bluetooth remote adds wireless pan, up/down, LED control and a real motor stop from Android or iPhone (Bluefy). Camera → Share to phone streams the AIRIZ camera to phones by link or 6-digit code over WebRTC, with an HTTPS frame relay (Cloudflare Worker) that keeps it working on company networks that block WebRTC. The browser and mobile-web editions remain available.
