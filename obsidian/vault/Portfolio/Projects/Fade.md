Barber marketplace with haircut shorts, live on the decentralised Fade Network or your own server: real accounts, end-to-end encrypted bookings and DMs, direct payments (PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Stripe links, bank/QR, Lightning) with a 0% fee, push notifications and 2 h reminders, video transcoding, comments and moderation with a moderator console, and 5–10% creator commissions when a short sends a booking.
