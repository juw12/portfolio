KiniBuka (“now open”) helps people find small Indonesian food and retail stalls <strong>that are open right now</strong>. A <strong>live feed</strong> shows openings, closings, new branches, promos and bazaars; <strong>Explore</strong> combines search, category filters and an OpenStreetMap <strong>map</strong>. Buyers <strong>follow</strong> stalls to get alerts and <strong>order ahead</strong> with a pickup time, tracking it from accepted to ready to done, with one-tap <strong>WhatsApp</strong> buttons to chat with the seller. Sellers get a dashboard to toggle open/closed, manage branches, menu and promos, run bazaars and handle incoming orders, with optional WhatsApp auto-notifications through Fonnte or the Meta Cloud API. Interface in <strong>Bahasa Indonesia, English and 中文</strong>. Plain Python server (no dependencies) on SQLite.
