Rewind is a community archive for <strong>old TV series that are hard to find on today’s streaming platforms</strong>. Browse the archive as poster tiles with search, decade and availability filters; each series opens a panel with <strong>memories</strong>, quotes, “I remember this” counts and a community <strong>availability status</strong> (not streaming, partial, physical media only, streaming). Keep favourites on <strong>My Shelf</strong> and follow new posts in <strong>Memory Lane</strong>. A full <strong>Settings</strong> page covers <strong>day/night mode</strong> (System, Light, Dark), accent colour, text size, card density, reduce motion, start page, default sort, hiding shows that are already streaming, and export/import of the archive. Every page and series has a <strong>shareable link</strong> and the back button closes panels. Runs <strong>local-first</strong> in the browser, or online with <strong>Supabase</strong> (magic-link sign-in, row-level security, realtime feed). Installable <strong>PWA</strong>, no build step.
