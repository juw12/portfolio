One home, one free account, three AI apps. <strong>GlowLead</strong>: give one goal and the AI lead plans it, hands each step to GlowChat or the GlowRoom crew, checks the work against clear done-criteria and reports back. <strong>GlowChat</strong>: chat with any model (ranked by category), your own persona, images, a usage meter, temporary chats, a Council with a Judge and an Arena. <strong>GlowRoom</strong>: an AI crew that searches the web, runs code and cites sources, in a warm Glow Studio office. One avatar in every app opens <strong>Profile & settings</strong>: name, Puter account, persona, token usage and allowance, privacy and your data. <strong>Speaks your language</strong>: pick English, Bahasa Indonesia, 简体中文 or 繁體中文 and your region on first open; the region decides which AI models you get (only providers that serve mainland China there, with PIPL consent and DeepSeek/Qwen/Kimi/GLM key presets), plus local prices and privacy notes. Free for guests.
