One home, one free account, three AI apps. <strong>GlowLead</strong>: give one goal and the AI lead plans it, hands each step to GlowChat or the GlowRoom crew, checks the work against clear done-criteria and reports back. <strong>GlowChat</strong>: chat with any model, personas, a Council with a Judge and an Arena. <strong>GlowRoom</strong>: an AI crew that searches the web, runs code and cites sources. Free for guests.
