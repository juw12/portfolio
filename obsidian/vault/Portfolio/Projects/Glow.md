One home, one free account, four AI apps. <strong>GlowLead</strong>: give one goal and the AI lead plans it, hands each step to GlowChat or the GlowRoom crew, checks the work against clear done-criteria and reports back. <strong>GlowChat</strong>: chat with any model (ranked by category), your own persona, images, a usage meter, temporary chats, a Council with a Judge and an Arena. <strong>GlowRoom</strong>: an AI crew that searches the web, runs code and cites sources, in a warm Glow Studio office. <strong>GlowNews</strong>: the day's AI news in one place, a new edition twice a day; the same story from many outlets becomes one card with every source, ranked and tagged by topic, with free-AI summaries, a 5-point briefing, “new for you” and one tap to discuss any story in GlowChat. <strong>Free AI built in</strong>: no keys or setup. Puter, OVHcloud (Llama 3.3 70B, GPT-OSS 120B, Qwen 3.5 397B, Mistral Small), LLM7 and Glow Free back each other up: when one is busy or used up, the next answers the same message, with the AI on your device as the last backup. One avatar in every app opens <strong>Profile & settings</strong>: name, Puter account, persona, token usage and allowance, privacy and your data. <strong>Speaks your language</strong>: pick English, Bahasa Indonesia, 简体中文 or 繁體中文 and your region on first open; the region decides which AI models you get (only providers that serve mainland China there, with PIPL consent), plus local prices and privacy notes. Free for guests.
