Hazel brings three creator tools under one name and one switcher. <strong>Hazel Shorts</strong> (formerly Shortcraft): write scenes, add your voice, and export a captioned 1080×1920 explainer with generated music, a post kit and YouTube upload, in English or Bahasa Indonesia. <strong>Hazel Sped</strong> (formerly Sped): turn your own songs into sped-up, slowed or mashup 9:16 shorts with a CapCut-style editor and MP4 export, fully offline. <strong>Hazel Long</strong> (the original Hazel): generate a storyboard from a prompt, refine it, package it for every platform — plus a Windows desktop app that auto-cuts silences and filler words with ffmpeg and Whisper. Local-first: no account, and existing projects from the old apps open as they were.
