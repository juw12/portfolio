A tiny animated pet that lives on your desktop and taskbar: <strong>23 animals</strong> in three groups — land animals walk, water animals swim and blow bubbles, sky animals hover and flap. Drag it anywhere, click to tickle it, rub to pet it; it has real moods (happy, sad, tired, hungry, grumpy), naps when you're away and greets you when you're back, and delivers break, water and eye-rest reminders in cute chat bubbles. Fully customisable colour, size and name, in English, Bahasa Indonesia, 繁體中文 and 简体中文. Click the pet to open <strong>Pips</strong>: an always-on-top Pomodoro timer with soundscapes, live app-usage tracking, a daily 1–5 star score, goals, streaks, a dashboard and history. Python + tkinter; everything stays local.
