A tiny animated pet that lives on your desktop: <strong>51 animals</strong> — land animals walk the taskbar and ride along on your open windows, water animals swim and leap out with a splash, sky animals fly anywhere and perch. Every animal has its own voice and song; pets have moods, grow up from baby to grown-up, catch a little cold when you skip breaks and show the live weather on your desktop. Click to tickle, double-click to open the Hub. <strong>Pips Friends</strong>: chat, groups and the Pips Community, pet visits, pokes, birthdays and gifts. <strong>Arena</strong>: turn-based skill battles (best of 1, 3 or 5) — press the skills yourself or switch on <strong>Auto</strong> and let your pet fight — plus minigames and a shop of hats, wings, moves and arena gear. Underneath, an always-on-top Pomodoro timer with soundscapes, app-usage tracking, a daily 1–5 star score, goals, streaks and history. English, Bahasa Indonesia, 繁體中文 and 简体中文. One-click Windows installer with in-app updates; focus history stays local, social features use the Pips online service.
