Live template marketplace for Excel / Google Sheets: describe a sheet in English or Bahasa Indonesia and Mint AI designs it, tweak it with words (“add column Supplier”), export a real .xlsx with live formulas and a dashboard, then sell it — buyers pay the seller directly — instantly through the seller's own Midtrans, Xendit or Stripe checkout (QRIS, e-wallets, VA, cards; the file unlocks by itself) or via their payment link with seller confirmation, with tracked affiliate commissions and payouts. Recovery codes, formula-free previews of paid templates, listing reports, order badges and Web Push notifications; Android app (APK) and iPhone Add to Home Screen. Cloudflare Workers + SQLite Durable Object backend.
