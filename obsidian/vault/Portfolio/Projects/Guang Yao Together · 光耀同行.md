An unofficial Traditional Chinese learning app for 發一光耀. Learners sign in with a free Puter account (progress syncs across devices) or continue as a guest; admins publish lessons, quizzes, handouts and events from a dedicated admin console backed by a versioned content repository. Includes an AI study companion that cites lessons and materials, quizzes, plans and makes flashcards, plus interactive lessons, spaced-repetition review, streaks, a usage guide and offline support. Also available as an Android app (APK download); iPhone users get a guided, free home-screen app, or can sideload the native app with a free Apple ID. Points, giving and chat remain demos; no payments or official certification.
