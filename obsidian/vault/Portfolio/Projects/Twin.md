Twin is an iPhone-ready investing and trading companion with a clean, professional design (1.5.1: new design system, line icons, dark and light themes) that updates itself the moment a new version ships (1.5.2). New in 1.6: the web demo covers about 280 stocks and ETFs on 35+ exchanges plus 34 indices from a dated snapshot; market status follows real exchange calendars (holidays, early closes); FX rates refresh every minute with their time shown; and real orders can go through Interactive Brokers for world exchanges as well as Alpaca for US listings. From 1.5: TwinAgent, a free research team of five analysts (trend, SEC-filed fundamentals, risk, relevance-screened headlines, famous investors' 13Fs) that argue a bull and bear case from cited, dated evidence; a portfolio manager rates the stock and a risk gate built from your own limits sizes or blocks the idea. No AI subscription, the same evidence always gives the same report, future data is filtered out, and a walk-forward scorecard tests the price evidence over decades against buy-and-hold. Search stocks by company, ticker or country — Tokyo, Hong Kong, Jakarta, London, Frankfurt, Mumbai, Toronto, São Paulo, Sydney and more — with exchange, currency, market status and next holidays, ≈ USD price with the FX time and US-listing hints on every stock page. World markets shows market clocks, 34 indices, movers and a heatmap; practice trades in a separate $10,000 USD portfolio convert foreign prices at current FX rates, and totals can be shown in your home currency (25 currencies). Continue as guest or share a ?guest link; market, limit, stop, stop-limit and trailing orders with brackets, price alerts, scrub charts with SMA/EMA/RSI, portfolio X-ray with country and currency exposure, a bar-by-bar Replay Lab and 12 Twin Academy lessons. The local server searches any Yahoo Finance listing; the web demo uses a dated snapshot of about 280 global stocks and ETFs. Optional real trading on the local server through your own Alpaca (US) or Interactive Brokers (world exchanges) account, with preview, confirm, caps and submission-time checks. Not financial advice.
